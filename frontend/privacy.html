Privacy

This is a personal portfolio site. It collects as little as possible. The summary below explains what happens when you visit or use the chat.

Last updated August 2026

What this site collects

As little as possible. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no tracking cookies. Like most websites, the hosting keeps standard access logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested page) for operational and security purposes; these are not used to profile visitors and are kept only as long as the hosting defaults require.

Analytics

None. No analytics product runs on this site: no Google Analytics, no pixels, no fingerprinting. If that ever changes, this page will name the product and link its privacy policy before it goes live.

The chat assistant

When you send a message in the chat widget, that message, along with the recent back-and-forth from your current tab, is sent to this site's chat service, which uses Anthropic's Claude to generate the reply. The conversation itself lives only in your browser tab and is gone when you close it; this site keeps no database of chat conversations. Anthropic processes the messages to produce the reply under its own privacy policy. Treat the chat like a public contact form: don't paste anything personal or confidential into it, ever.

Links out

This site links to third-party services including LinkedIn, GitHub, Credly, and Microsoft Learn. Once you follow one of those links, that service’s own privacy policy applies, not this one.
